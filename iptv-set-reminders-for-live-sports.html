How to Use IPTV Features to Set Reminders for Live Sports

Published: October 23, 2026 Author: B1G IPTV 5 Min Read

Missing the start of a big game is frustrating—especially when you have access to thousands of live sports channels with a premium IPTV service like B1G IPTV. The good news is that most modern IPTV apps and supported devices offer built-in features to set reminders for upcoming live sports events. With the right setup, your TV or mobile device can notify you before kickoff, tip-off, or first pitch, so you’re always ready when the action begins.

Understanding Reminder Features in IPTV Apps

Most IPTV apps compatible with B1G IPTV—such as TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, and Smart IPTV—include features to help you track and schedule live events. These reminder options typically integrate with the Electronic Program Guide (EPG), displaying upcoming events and allowing you to set alerts or notifications for specific programs.

  • Program Guide (EPG): Lets you browse upcoming matches and events chronologically.
  • Reminders: Lets you mark specific games to receive an alert before they start.
  • Favorites: Allows quick access to teams, leagues, or channels you watch often.

Step-by-Step: Setting Reminders for Live Sports

The exact steps will depend on your IPTV app and device, but the general process is similar across platforms. Below is a guide for several popular apps:

On TiviMate (Android TV/Firestick)

  1. Open the TiviMate app and ensure your B1G IPTV subscription is active.
  2. Navigate to the EPG or TV Guide section.
  3. Scroll to the date and time of the live sports event you want to watch.
  4. Select the event or match. In the event details window, choose “Set Reminder” or “Add to Calendar.”
  5. TiviMate will notify you on-screen a few minutes before the event starts.

On IPTV Smarters (Smart TVs, Android, iOS)

  1. Launch IPTV Smarters and sign in with your B1G IPTV credentials.
  2. Go to the EPG or Live TV section.
  3. Find your desired sports event and select it.
  4. Tap or click the “Set Reminder” icon (usually a bell or calendar symbol).
  5. You’ll get a pop-up or push notification on your device before the event begins.

On Smart IPTV (Samsung, LG, Android TVs)

  1. Open Smart IPTV and load your B1G IPTV playlist.
  2. Access the program guide and locate the live sports broadcast.
  3. Press the OK or select button to view program info.
  4. Choose “Set Reminder” or similar (feature availability may vary by model).
  5. Your TV will display a reminder banner at the scheduled time.

Maximizing Reminders: Tips and Best Practices

To get the most out of reminders for live sports, consider these practical tips:

  • Keep Your EPG Updated: Refresh your program guide regularly to ensure you see the latest schedules. EPG data is typically updated when you restart the app or refresh manually in the settings.
  • Use Device Notifications: On mobile devices, make sure app notifications are enabled in your phone or tablet settings. On Smart TVs, check that pop-ups or alerts are allowed.
  • Add to Calendar: Some IPTV apps let you export events to your Google or Apple calendar for additional reminders.
  • Set Multiple Reminders: For critical games, set reminders both in your IPTV app and your device’s native calendar.
  • Mark Favorites: Add favorite teams or sports channels in your app so you can quickly find upcoming games and set reminders.

Troubleshooting: When Reminders Don’t Work

If you’re not receiving reminders as expected, here are steps to resolve common issues:

  • Check App Permissions: Ensure your IPTV app is allowed to send notifications. Update settings in your device’s app management area.
  • Update Your App: Outdated apps may not support all features. Download the latest version from your device’s app store.
  • Restart Devices: Sometimes, a simple restart of your TV, streaming device, or app can restore reminder functions.
  • Review EPG Source: If the guide data is missing or incomplete, reminders may not be available for all events. Refresh or reload your EPG source as needed.
  • Contact Support: If problems persist, B1G IPTV’s support team can assist by email or WhatsApp with troubleshooting your device and app settings.
