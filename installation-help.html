Amazon Fire TV Stick, Fire TV 4K and Cube

Firestick and Fire TV make it easy to stream 4K sports and movies from B1G IPTV—just enter your server details from our email in your chosen app.

IPTV Smarters ProTiviMateDownloader
1

Install the Downloader app

From your Firestick Home screen go to Find > Search. Type Downloader, choose the orange Downloader icon in the Amazon Appstore and select Get.

2

Allow Downloader to install apps

Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options. Select Install Unknown Apps and turn Downloader to ON.

Do not see Developer Options? Go to Settings > My Fire TV > About, highlight your Fire TV name and press the centre Select button 7 times until you see "You are now a developer".
3

Install a player app

Search for IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate in the Appstore, or install the app through Downloader.

4

Log in with Xtream Codes API

Open the app and choose Xtream Codes API. Enter the server URL, username and password from your confirmation email, then select Add. Your channels, TV guide (EPG) and movies load right away.

Smart TVs: Samsung Tizen, LG webOS and Hisense

Samsung and LG Smart TVs can run B1G IPTV using built-in IPTV apps—set up is easy with the login and server details you receive by email.

IBO PlayerSmart IPTVNanomid
1

Install the player from the TV app store

On your Samsung Smart Hub or LG Content Store, search for IBO Player (or Smart IPTV) and select Install.

2

Find your Device MAC and Key

Open the app on your television. The home screen shows your TV's Device MAC Address (for example b4:0e:22:xx:xx:xx) and the Device Key.

3

Sign in on a computer or phone

On your computer or smartphone open the official portal https://iboplayer.com/device/login. Enter your Device MAC and Key, then select Login.

4

Add your Xtream playlist

Select Add XC Playlist, enter a playlist name, the server URL, username and password from your confirmation email, then select Save. Restart the app on your TV to watch right away.

Android TV, Google TV, Nvidia Shield and Android boxes

Android TV boxes and phones offer a flexible way to watch—use any popular IPTV app, add your credentials, and get streaming in minutes.

TiviMate IPTV PlayerIPTV Smarters Pro
1

Install a player from Google Play

Search for TiviMate IPTV Player or IPTV Smarters Pro in the Google Play Store and install it.

2

Choose Xtream Codes API

Open the app, select Add Playlist, then choose Xtream Codes API.

3

Enter your details

Type the server URL, username and password from your confirmation email, then select Next.

4

Let the guide load

The app downloads your live channels, TV guide (EPG), movies and series. Open any channel to start watching.

Apple iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Apple TV

Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad run B1G IPTV through IPTV-compatible apps. Add the server link and login sent by us and you’re ready to start.

IPTV Smarters ProGSE Smart IPTVIBO Player
1

Install from the App Store

Open the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Apple TV 4K. Search for Smarters Player Lite (or IBO Player or GSE Smart IPTV) and tap Get.

2

Open the app and accept the terms

Launch the app on your Apple device and accept the user agreement.

3

Log in with Xtream Codes API

Choose Login with Xtream Codes API. Enter any name, your username, password and server URL.

4

Watch anywhere

Tap Add User. You can watch on the device or cast instantly to any AirPlay television.

Windows PC, laptop and Mac

Windows and Mac computers can stream over IPTV apps—just copy in the server, username, and password from your welcome email.

IPTV Smarters Pro DesktopVLC Media Player
1

Option A: IPTV Smarters Pro (recommended)

Download IPTV Smarters Pro for Windows (.exe) or Mac (.dmg). Open the app, choose Xtream Codes API, enter your username, password and server URL, then select Add User.

2

Option B: VLC Media Player (quick stream)

Open VLC and press Ctrl + N on Windows or Cmd + N on Mac to open Network Stream. Paste the M3U link from your confirmation email and select Play.

MAG boxes (250, 254, 322, 420, 524) and Formuler

MAG boxes are supported by B1G IPTV—set your portal with the server link from email and enjoy full live sports and on demand viewing.

Built-in portalMyTVOnline
1

Find your MAC address

The MAC address starts with 00:1A:79 and is printed on the label under the box. You can also find it in Settings > System Settings > Device Info.

2

Send us your MAC address

Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your MAC address so we can activate your box.

3

Enter the portal

On your box open Settings > System Settings > Servers > Portals. Set Portal 1 Name to your brand and Portal 1 URL to the portal URL from your confirmation email.

4

Save and restart

Save, go back and choose Reboot Device. The box loads your TV guide after it restarts.

Roku sticks and Roku TV

To watch on Roku, use an IPTV player channel and your B1G IPTV login details—we provide email support to guide you through the setup.

Screen mirroringApple AirPlayWeb Video Caster
1

Option A: AirPlay or screen mirroring (fastest)

Play a channel in IPTV Smarters on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. Tap the Cast or AirPlay icon and choose your Roku. Playback starts on the big screen.

2

Option B: Web Video Caster

Install Web Video Caster on your phone and on your Roku. Paste the M3U link from your confirmation email, choose your Roku and press play.

Roku does not run IPTV player apps directly, so casting is the way to watch on it.
